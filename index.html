How this stays live

  1. This page fetches your Google Sheet directly — "Refresh Now" pulls it instantly, or pick an auto-refresh interval above and it re-pulls on that schedule while this page is open.
  2. The sheet must stay shared as "Anyone with the link – Viewer" for the fetch to work. If you ever see a sync error below, that setting is the first thing to check.
  3. Just edit the sheet as usual — no separate "tell Claude" step needed anymore for routine trades. Reopening this page always starts from the interval above (5 min by default); it doesn't remember your last choice between visits.
