Score = criteria passed (1 pt) + partial (½ pt), out of the criteria with available data for that symbol — a symbol with data gaps is scored out of a smaller total rather than penalized for the gap. 3 criteria from the original screening checklist (Earnings Surprise History, New Product/Catalyst, Sector/Industry Strength) aren't automatable from available data and are excluded from the score entirely — shown below for reference. Stock Portfolio NAV vs Market Indices — indexed to 100, adjusted for money added or withdrawn; stocks only, ETFs excluded

Building history — this chart plots your stock portfolio's value over time (ETFs excluded — they're a cushion, not the benchmark) against Nifty 50, Nifty 500, Nifty Midcap 150, and Nifty 200. It needs at least 2 days of snapshots in the History tab; check back after tomorrow's snapshot lands, or ask Claude for the one-time setup script if this is a fresh sheet.

XIRR Performance — money-weighted annualized return, Total vs Stocks vs ETFs, snapshotted once a day

Building XIRR history — this chart plots your Total, Stock, and ETF XIRR (annualized, money-weighted return) over time so you can see which side of the portfolio is performing better. It needs at least 2 daily snapshots with a computable XIRR in the XirrHistory tab (each holding must be 30+ days old before an annualized rate can be computed) — check back once both conditions are met. Note: this can't be backfilled — each day's figures reflect that day's holdings, so the series only starts accumulating from whenever this feature was first set up.

How this stays live

  1. This page fetches your Google Sheet directly — "Refresh Now" pulls it instantly, or pick an auto-refresh interval above and it re-pulls on that schedule while this page is open.
  2. The sheet must stay shared as "Anyone with the link – Viewer" for the fetch to work. If you ever see a sync error below, that setting is the first thing to check.
  3. Just edit the sheet as usual — no separate "tell Claude" step needed anymore for routine trades. Reopening this page always starts from the interval above (5 min by default); it doesn't remember your last choice between visits.
